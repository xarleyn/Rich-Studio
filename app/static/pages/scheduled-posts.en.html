Schedule posts for Telegram channels

Prepare a post ahead of time and choose when to publish. The RichPost server sends it, so you can close the app after saving the schedule.

Add a post to the queue

  1. Sign in with Telegram. Connect a channel you manage: the bot needs administrator status and permission to post.
  2. Create the post and check its text, media and buttons. Send a preview to your direct messages if needed.
  3. Open Channels in the header. Select one or several destinations and check the list.
  4. Open the menu beside Publish and choose Schedule. Pick a future time and confirm.
  5. Open Posts → Scheduled. Check the time and channel for every entry.

Time zones and changes

The scheduling dialog uses your browser's time zone and displays its name beside the time picker. Check it before confirming if you travel or use a remote computer.

Open a scheduled entry from Posts to edit it. Update post saves new content to that entry; Reschedule changes its time. Publishing from the editor creates a new post, so check which action you choose.

Review the outcome

After the scheduled time, check the status in Posts and open the channel in Telegram. The server must be running and channel permissions must remain valid. If the service was offline too long, the post may be marked missed: schedule it again.

After an ambiguous network error, a post may already have reached Telegram. The studio does not automatically retry that send. Check the channel before publishing again to avoid a duplicate.

For multiple channels, check each outcome: a successful send to one channel does not imply delivery to the others.
