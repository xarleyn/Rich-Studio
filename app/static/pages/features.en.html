Where to start

  1. Sign in with Telegram and create a draft. Start with the Markdown guide if you want to work with source text.
  2. Connect your channel and give the bot permission to post. Check the preview and send a private test before an important publication.
  3. Choose immediate or scheduled publishing. Afterwards, check the outcome in Posts and in the channel itself.

The guides describe rich messages in RichPost. The Telegram client also affects their appearance. Agent tools can work with your account only after a connection with a personal token is configured.
