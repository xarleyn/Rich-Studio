Your agent can prepare drafts, show previews and fill your post templates. Continue editing the same posts in RichPost whenever you like.

What to ask your agent

Copy this message into Codex, Claude Code or another client that supports remote MCP connections:

Connect RichPost for me. Read the setup guide at __AGENT_GUIDE_URL__, configure MCP and install the skill if my client supports it. Ask me to issue a personal token. Then check that the connection and tools work.

You can also share a link to this page. Its setup documents are readable without signing in.

One step is yours: issue a token

  1. Sign in to RichPost through Telegram.
  2. Open your account menu → API and MCP tokens → Create token. Choose a client name and an expiry.
  3. Choose Drafts for writing and templates. Use Read only for viewing, or Publishing when you want to allow your agent to send posts.
  4. Save the token, shown once, in your client's secret settings following your agent's instructions. Keep it out of public chats and repositories.

The agent does not issue its own token. You can revoke it in the same menu. Publishing permission is separate from your request to send a particular post: agree on its content and destination first.

Check that the connection works

Your agent should verify the connection and available tools, then report the result. Your client may need a restart or confirmation for the new connection. A client that supports only OAuth login needs a different way to connect with a personal token.

After setup, try “Prepare an announcement draft”, “Show me a preview”, or “Create a post from my template”. To publish, connect your channel in the editor and grant the bot the required permissions.
