Before your first post

A few good questions.

Do I need a password?

No. Confirm sign-in with Telegram. Each account has its own drafts, files, connected channels and scheduled posts.

Which channel permissions are required?

You must be the owner or an administrator allowed to post messages. The bot also needs administrator status and posting permission. Your current permissions are checked again before channel operations.

Can I publish to several channels?

Yes. Connect your channels and select them for the post. You can publish only to channels connected to your account.

Does my computer need to stay on for scheduled posts?

No. The server sends scheduled posts. Once you choose a time, you can close the app. Check the queue and delivery outcome in Posts.

What data does the service store?

The server stores Telegram sign-in data, drafts, uploaded files, connected channels, history and the publication queue. Each account has separate storage. Content is sent to Telegram when you request a direct-message preview or publish. Connect only channels you manage; you can revoke the bot's access in your Telegram channel settings.
