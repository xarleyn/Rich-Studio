Before your first post

Clear answers to common questions

What you need to get started and how the service handles your data.

How is RichPost different from a regular message?

You can build a rich post with headings, tables, lists, quotes, media and buttons, then check it in the preview.

Do I need to create a password?

No. Sign in is confirmed with Telegram. Each account has its own drafts, files, connected channels and scheduled posts.

Which channel permissions are required?

You must be the owner or an administrator allowed to post. The bot also needs administrator status and posting permission. Permissions are checked again before channel operations.

Can I publish to several channels?

Yes. Connect your channels and select them for the post. You can publish only to channels connected to your account.

Does my computer need to stay on?

No. The server sends scheduled posts. Once you choose a time, you can close the app. Check the queue and delivery outcome in Posts.

What data does the service store?

The server stores Telegram sign in data, drafts, uploaded files, connected channels, history and the publication queue. Each account has separate storage. You can revoke the bot's access in your channel settings.
